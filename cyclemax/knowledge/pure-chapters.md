# PURE – freigegebene Kapitel (brinkmannpaul.com, „system for man“)

Pauls freigegebener englischer Wortlaut, gekürzt auf die für Cyclemax relevanten Stellen.

## responsibility
responsibility is not just a weight you carry. it is power you choose to take.
bring that same responsibility into your relationship and your family. keep your word. communicate clearly.
say what you want, what you do not want and what you are prepared to commit to. when you make a mistake,
acknowledge it and do the work to repair it.
responsibility has nothing to do with possessing people. your partner is not your property. protecting your
family does not mean controlling them.

## ego
for me, true masculinity means letting go of that need to prove yourself. you do not need to shout, make
yourself look bigger or dominate every conversation. you can accept people as they are and listen to an
opinion that is different from your own without feeling threatened.
acceptance does not mean agreement. it also does not mean allowing people to cross your boundaries.
i believe your boundaries need foundations as solid as concrete. know what matters to you. make it clear.
when someone crosses a boundary, you should be able to say calmly: "this crosses my boundary. i do not want this."
you can be firm without humiliating someone. you can listen without surrendering your values. you can change
your mind without losing your dignity.
as a man, it is your responsibility to make yourself known and to take the time to know your partner. do not
expect her to understand boundaries you have never expressed. do not assume you understand hers without listening.
when your ego no longer needs to win every disagreement, there is more room to understand your wife, honour
her and protect the family you are building together.
freedom begins when you understand that something greater and more intelligent than you exists. you are not
above everything and do not need to be.

## understanding the mind
a thought appears. a desire follows. you feel an urge to do something. but feeling an urge does not mean you
have to follow it.

## physical control
you learn when to push, when to recover and when to say no. understanding your body gives you a foundation for
leading your life.
