# Notes for App Review (Apple / Google)

**No account or login is required.** The app works immediately after a short onboarding.

**What Cyclemax is:** a coaching app for men in relationships (attitude, calm communication, leadership by example), based on
the PURE Method by Paul Brinkmann and Stoic philosophy. The man enters only one thing: the first day of his partner's period,
when she tells him or he asks her. The app then shows him each day which of four phases is likely, what can come in that
phase (general, hedged information with sources on the "Verstehen" screen) and how he stays calm. It is not a period
tracker for women, not a contraception, fertility or health app, makes no diagnoses and never predicts fertile days.

What the app does natively (beyond a website):
- **Local notifications** (`@capacitor/local-notifications`): max. one per day, scheduled entirely on the device for the next
  30 days (iOS limit of 64 pending notifications respected, refilled on every app start). Cycle data never leaves the device for this.
- **Speech input** (own Swift plugin on `SFSpeechRecognizer`, on-device recognition when supported): he can talk instead of type
  in the chat and in "Erzähl mir von euch". The app stores and sends no audio. Permission texts are in Info.plist.
- **Offline**: the full app (UI, phase engine, knowledge base) is bundled; the mentor chat falls back to on-device content without network.
- **Local storage** (`@capacitor/preferences`): cycle data, profile and chat history stay on the device.
- **Haptics** on the one-tap entry and on thumbs up/down.
- **Delete all data** in Settings removes local data and all server data linked to the random device id (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).

AI: chat answers are generated by Claude (Anthropic) via our server. Users can rate answers and report them anonymously;
reports are reviewed in an admin panel. Help resources (emergency numbers) are attached automatically for messages about
violence or self-harm. Content rules: no sexual content, no manipulation tactics, no demeaning statements about women.

How to test (about one minute):
1. "Weiter" → "Beziehung" → pick a date about 3 weeks ago → "Weiter" → "Später" (or allow notifications).
2. Home shows the phase ring (e.g. "Standfest"), one sentence of attitude and "Was kommen kann". Tap the ring → "Verstehen"
   with the next phases and dates.
3. Back → "Cyclemax fragen" → tap a quick question or type "Wie soll ich antworten?". The microphone button tests speech input.
4. "Ihre Tage haben heute begonnen" → the ring turns to "Wärme"; "Rückgängig" undoes it.
5. Settings (top right) → "Alle Daten löschen".
