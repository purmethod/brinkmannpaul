# What a Good Man Actually Is

> Status: **Buchtext steht.** Schlusskapitel der Säule ego.

## Kernaussagen (Paul)

- Ein guter Mann ist nicht unschuldig. Er ist klar.
- Er hält sein Feuer, hält sein Wort, führt ohne Gewalt und steht für etwas, das größer ist als er selbst.
- Ein echter Mann ist Stabilität. Der Fels in der Brandung.
- Ich wurde der Bruder, den ich nie hatte.

## Buchtext (EN)

Forget enlightenment. The word has been chewed to nothing by people who could not define it if you held them to it.

A good man is a man whose soul is **quiet**.

And a soul gets quiet exactly one way: by having walked into the four arenas, having gotten up off the floor at least once, and having looked directly at its own failures without running.

Not innocent. **Clear.**

He holds his fire, keeps his word, leads without force and stands for something greater than himself.

That is the 1%. Not a floor above the others. Outside the building entirely, where a man can look at every level of that structure, the sleeping, the striving, the powerful, and feel no need to be anywhere in it.

Now ask the question again.

**Do you think you belong to the 1%?**

And this time, before you answer: which arenas have you actually entered, and what did you take responsibility for when you came out?
