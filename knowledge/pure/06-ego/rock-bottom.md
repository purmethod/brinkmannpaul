# Rock Bottom

> Status: **Buchtext steht.** Pauls Korrektur ist verbindlich: Rock Bottom ist ein dramatisches Wort und wird nicht weichgezeichnet. Es braucht keine Katastrophe, aber es ist auch kein sanfter Wendepunkt.

## Kernaussagen (Paul)

- Rock Bottom ist ein dramatisches Wort, und das soll es sein. Es ist der Boden unter allem.
- Bei manchen ist es in der Kindheit passiert: ein gewalttätiger Vater, ein alkoholkranker Vater, eine Mutter, die gegangen ist. Bei manchen wartet es noch in der Zukunft.
- Du musst deines identifizieren.
- Ein Kind, das verlassen wurde, trägt die Verlustangst in jede Beziehung, bis es sie benennt. Bis du es benennst, steuert es dich.

## Buchtext (EN)

Somewhere in those four arenas, you will hit it. All of us do.

Rock bottom is a dramatic word. I am not going to soften it.

It is dramatic, it is painful, and it is exactly what it sounds like: the floor beneath everything, the place with nothing left underneath. Anyone who tells you it is a gentle turning point has never been there.

And you have to find yours. Not because pain is noble. Because until you have identified it, it is running your life from a place you cannot see.

### It May Already Be Behind You

Most men look for rock bottom in the future, or in the obvious places: the business that failed, the divorce, the year everything came apart. For some men that is where it is.

But for many, **rock bottom happened before they were old enough to name it.**

Your father beat you. Your father was a choleric and the house went silent when his car pulled up. Your father drank himself into cancer and was gone by fifty. Your mother left.

That is where it lives for more men than anyone admits: in a childhood they have filed as normal because it was the only one they had.

### Why You Have to Name It

The mechanism is not mystical. If you were abandoned as a small child, it traumatizes you. Not for a season. For life, until the day you turn around and look at it directly. And it comes out in your relationships every single time, in ways that feel like personality but are a child's nervous system still running its emergency program.

**You cannot fix what you have not named.** That is the entire reason this section exists.

### Finding Yours

Every man has one. Some have several, one in each arena.

Yours might be your father. A bed you lay in as a child listening to something you were too young to understand. The year the money went. The person who is not coming back.

Or it has not happened yet. If you are young, or life has been kind so far, understand clearly: **rock bottom may still be waiting for you.** That is not a threat, it is preparation. The man who knows it is coming meets it differently than the man who thought he was exempt.

But if it has already happened, and for most men reading this it has, your work is not to survive it. You already did that. Your work is to **identify** it, name it out loud, and trace the exact lines running from that event into how you handle money, women, trust and loss today.

## Belege

- **Die Kindheit wirkt ein Leben lang.** In der großen ACE-Studie mit über 9.000 auswertbaren Erwachsenen hingen belastende Kindheitserfahrungen (Misshandlung, Gewalt im Haushalt, Sucht oder psychische Erkrankung eines Elternteils, Trennung) stufenweise mit mehr Krankheit, Sucht und Risikoverhalten im Erwachsenenalter zusammen. Je mehr solcher Erfahrungen, desto größer das Risiko. Pauls Satz „it happened before they were old enough to name it“ ist genau das. [Felitti et al. 1998]
- **Benennen ist der erste Eingriff.** Gefühle in Worte zu fassen dämpft die Alarmreaktion im Gehirn. [Lieberman et al. 2007]
