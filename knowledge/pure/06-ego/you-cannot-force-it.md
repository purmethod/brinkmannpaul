## Kernaussagen (Paul)

- Du kannst dich nicht zwingen. Ein erzwungener Anfang ist eine Lüge.
- Du entfernst die Sucht nicht. Du ersetzt sie. Du tauschst die Identität und ersetzt eine Leidenschaft durch eine andere.
- Besser süchtig nach Training als nach Alkohol. Besser süchtig nach Aufbauen als nach einem Bildschirm.

## Buchtext (EN)

Now the lie every self-help book on the shelf is built on.

**You cannot force yourself.**

You do not wake up one morning and decide you are done smoking. You do not stand up off the floor and announce that from today you are not an alcoholic. You do not close the laptop on the porn and declare it finished. Every man who has tried knows how it ends: three days, maybe eleven, and then you are back, now carrying the extra weight of having failed at something you swore.

Willpower is not the mechanism. Willpower is a battery, and it runs out on schedule: usually in the evening, usually exactly when you need it.

**You do not remove the addiction. You replace it.**

### The Swap

Read that again, because it is the whole thing.

You are not going to become a man with no compulsion. That man does not exist. You have a nervous system that wants a reward and it is going to get one somewhere. The question was never *whether* you will be obsessed. The question is **what with**.

Better obsessed with training than with alcohol. Better addicted to building something than to a screen. Better compulsive about cold water than about the next drink.

You do not fight the drive. **You redirect it.**

The word for a redirected drive is **passion**. Not a soft word here. Passion is what pulls a man out of every other addiction. Not discipline, not shame, not a promise made at three in the morning. Something else has to become louder than the old thing. Nothing else gets a man out.

### The Identity Is the Real Lever

Underneath the swap is something deeper, and most men never go there.

You have to change **who you are**, not what you do.

As long as you are an alcoholic who is not drinking today, you are an alcoholic and the clock is running. As long as you are a porn user on day nine, you are a porn user on day nine. The behavior changed and the identity did not, and identity always wins, because a man returns to who he believes he is the way water finds level.

The shift is not "I am quitting." It is **"I am not a man who does that."**

That sounds like semantics until you have lived both. One is a man holding a door shut with his shoulder. The other is a man for whom the door is not interesting.

And identity does not change by declaration either. It changes by accumulation: a long enough run of small actions that the brain finally updates the file. Slowly. The way a body adapts to cold water, not the way you flip a switch.

### So Read the Instruction Again

Earlier I said: do not stay down. That stands. But now you know what it means, and it is not what the motivation industry sells you.

**Getting up is not a decision made with your jaw clenched. Getting up is a swap.**

Find the one thing you can become obsessed with that builds you instead of eating you, then climb toward it in steps small enough that you cannot fail, until your system starts pulling you on its own.

Not force. **Replacement.**
Not "I quit." **"I am not that man."**

## Belege

- **Willenskraft ist der schwächere Hebel.** Situation verändern wirkt besser als im Moment zu widerstehen. [Duckworth et al. 2016]
- **Identität ändert sich durch Wiederholung.** Automatik entsteht im Schnitt nach 66 Tagen konsequenter Wiederholung. [Lally et al. 2010]
